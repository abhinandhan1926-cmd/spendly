Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly (the "Service") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our personal expense tracking application. By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly when using the Service, including your account details (such as name and email address) and the financial data you choose to log, such as expense amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical information, like browser type and access times, to help us maintain and improve the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data you provide to operate the Service — for example, to display your expense history, generate spending summaries, and keep your account secure. We may also use aggregated, non-identifying data to understand usage patterns and improve the Service. We do not use your personal financial data for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a database associated with your account and is retained for as long as your account remains active. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell your personal data to third parties. We may rely on trusted third-party providers for infrastructure needs such as hosting or analytics; these providers only process data as necessary to support the Service and are bound by their own confidentiality and security obligations. We do not share your individual expense data with advertisers.

5. Your Choices

You can review, update, or delete the information in your account at any time by using the features available within the Service. If you have questions about the data we hold about you, you are welcome to contact us using the details below.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact channels provided within the Service.
